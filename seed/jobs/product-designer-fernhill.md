Our customers aren't accountants and don't want to become one. They're illustrators, developers, translators and two-person studios. They open Fernhill once a month, answer a few questions and close it again, and once a year it files their tax return. Your job is to keep it that simple as the product grows.

We have two designers, and you'd be the third, working across the product with our engineers and our product manager. This year we're adding VAT returns in two more countries and a way for an accountant to look over a customer's shoulder, and both have to fit into those ten minutes a month.

## What you'll do

- Design new flows end to end, from the first sketch to the shipped screen.
- Talk to five customers a month, and watch them use the product.
- Write the words on the screens with us: in a tax product, the words are most of the design.
- Look after our small design system with the frontend team.
- Measure whether what we shipped worked, and come back to it when it didn't.

## About you

- A portfolio that shows how you got to the design, not only the result.
- Comfortable with forms, tables and numbers; you think empty states are interesting.
- You can prototype in Figma, and you're not afraid of a little code.
- You care about accessibility as much as how things look.

## Nice to have

- You've designed for finance, tax or other regulated products.
- You've freelanced, and dreaded your own tax return.

## How we work

Remote in Europe, within two hours of Dublin time. The design team reviews work together twice a week, and we meet as a company for a week each year in the west of Ireland.

## What we offer

- 30 days off, plus public holidays where you live.
- A new laptop of your choice.
- A budget of €1,500 a year for courses and books.
- An accountant (ours) for your own taxes.

## How we hire

1. A call with our head of design.
2. A portfolio walk-through: one project, in depth.
3. A paid half-day design exercise on a real problem, talked through with the team.
