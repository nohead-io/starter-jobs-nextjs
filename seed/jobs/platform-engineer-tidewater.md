Tidewater answers about forty million forecast requests a day, from harbour offices, ferry operators and sailing apps. The forecasts are rebuilt every hour from new model runs, and nobody calling the API may notice: answers stay under 100 milliseconds while the data behind them is replaced.

Our platform team is two engineers, and you'd be the third. You'll look after everything between the forecasting models and the customers' requests: the clusters, the deploys, the data pipeline and the tools the on-call engineer reaches for at night.

## What you'll do

- Run our Kubernetes clusters in three regions, and simplify them where you can.
- Own deploys, observability and the on-call rotation's tooling.
- Work with the forecasting team to get new models into production safely, with a way back.
- Keep our cloud bill in proportion: the hourly model runs are expensive.
- Take your turn on call, one week in four, and make it quieter each time.

## About you

- Several years running production infrastructure on AWS or GCP.
- Terraform, Kubernetes and a healthy suspicion of both.
- You can write a service as well as run one, ideally in Go or Python.
- Clear writing: most of our decisions are made in documents.

## Nice to have

- Experience with large data pipelines or scientific computing.
- You've reduced a cloud bill without anyone noticing.

## How we work

Work from anywhere with four hours of overlap with UTC. Documents first, meetings when they help, and an incident review after every page that woke someone up.

## What we offer

- 28 days off, plus public holidays where you live.
- An on-call allowance, and a day off after a rough night.
- A home-office budget.
- One sailing trip a year, which is research.

## How we hire

1. A call with our CTO.
2. A design conversation: how you'd run a system like ours, on a whiteboard.
3. A conversation about an incident you were part of, with two engineers.
