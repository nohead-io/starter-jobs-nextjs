Every country in Europe pays people differently. Tax, social security, pensions and holiday pay all follow their own rules, and the rules change every January and often in between. Our product has to follow every rule, and change on the day a rule does.

You'll join our payroll team: three specialists who each own a group of countries, and the eight engineers who build what they specify. Your countries are Ireland, the Netherlands and Germany, where most of our new customers come from.

## What you'll do

- Own the payroll rules for Ireland, the Netherlands and Germany.
- Write the specifications engineers build from, with worked examples, and test what they build.
- Follow every change in the law, and plan for it before it lands.
- Check the first payroll runs for new customers in your countries, and explain any surprises.
- Answer the hardest payroll questions from support, and turn the answers into help articles.

## About you

- Experience running payroll or building payroll software in at least one of your countries.
- A careful reader of legislation, and a clear writer.
- You enjoy the edge cases: a leaver in the middle of a month, a backdated pay rise.
- You're comfortable with spreadsheets, and curious about the software behind them.

## Nice to have

- German or Dutch.
- Experience with pension rules in more than one country.

## How we work

Remote in Europe, across seven countries, with most of the day overlapping. January and the weeks around payday are busy; we plan holidays around them together. We meet as a company twice a year.

## What we offer

- A salary from our published bands, the same for everyone at the same level.
- 30 days off, plus public holidays where you live.
- €1,500 a year for courses and memberships in your field.
- Payroll that's never late.

## How we hire

1. A call with our head of payroll.
2. A short exercise: a specification for a rule change, from the law to an example payslip.
3. A conversation with an engineer and another payroll specialist.
