Quillmark is an editor for long documents written together. Our customers write handbooks, grant applications and whole books in it, often with a dozen people in the same document, and some of those documents are three hundred pages long.

The editor is the product. We have four frontend engineers working on it, alongside two designers, and we'd like a fifth. Most of our work is making things that sound simple, such as typing, selecting text and leaving a comment, keep working perfectly when the document is huge and someone else is editing the same paragraph.

## What you'll do

- Work on the editor: typing, selection, comments, tracked changes and real-time collaboration.
- Keep it fast on long documents and old laptops, and prove it with measurements.
- Build the screens around the editor in React: the outline, the history, sharing and review.
- Help shape how we test an editor, which is harder than it sounds.
- Pair with the designers on the details: how a cursor moves, what an undo undoes.

## About you

- Strong TypeScript and React; you've built something rich in the browser.
- You've worked with ProseMirror, Lexical or similar, or want to learn it well.
- You care about accessibility, including in places where it's hard, such as an editor.
- You read the browser's specifications when the documentation runs out.

## Nice to have

- Experience with CRDTs or another approach to collaborative editing.
- You've worked on performance in large web apps.

## How we work

Remote in Europe, working mostly in writing, with one planning call a week. Every engineer spends a few hours a month reading support conversations. We meet as a company three times a year.

## What we offer

- A four-day week in August, at full pay.
- 28 days off, plus public holidays where you live.
- €2,000 a year for learning.
- The laptop of your choice, and a budget for your desk and chair.

## How we hire

1. A call with our head of engineering.
2. A pairing session on a small part of a real editor. No take-home work.
3. A conversation with a designer and two engineers about how you work.
