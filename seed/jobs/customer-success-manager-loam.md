Winter is when growers plan, and when they have time to learn a new tool. Once spring comes, they're in the field from dawn and won't read an email until October. We'd like someone to help them get going before the season starts.

Loam is a team of nine, based in Bristol. Most of our customers are small market gardens and family farms in the UK, growing anything from salad leaves to forty varieties of squash. Many of them have kept their records in a notebook for twenty years, and switching is a big step.

## What you'll do

- Run onboarding calls with new farms, and visit some of them in the South West.
- Help growers move their records over from notebooks and spreadsheets, and set up their fields and crops.
- Prepare farms for their organic inspection, so the paperwork comes out of Loam on the day.
- Write the guides growers ask for, in plain words, with photos taken in real fields.
- Collect what growers need and tell the product team, with names and examples.
- Run a short webinar each winter month, for growers who'd rather learn in a group.

## About you

- You've worked with farmers or growers, or grown things yourself, beyond a window box.
- Friendly, organised and happy on the phone, including with people who don't like computers.
- You write clearly, and you can explain one thing three different ways.
- A driving licence: some farms are a long way from a station.

## How we work

Three days a week, in our office in Bristol on Tuesdays and Wednesdays and from home on the third. Busy in winter and early spring, quieter in summer, and we plan holidays around that together.

## What we offer

- A salary for three days a week. The range above is for those three days.
- Pro-rata 28 days off, plus bank holidays.
- Travel to farms paid, and a day off after a long one.
- A share of the veg box, from whichever customer is growing the most that week.

## How we hire

1. A call with Hannah, who runs customer success, about you and the role.
2. A practice onboarding call, with one of us playing a grower.
3. A visit to a farm with the team, and lunch.
