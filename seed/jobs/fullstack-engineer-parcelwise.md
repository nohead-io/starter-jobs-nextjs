Parcelwise connects four thousand small online shops to eleven carriers. Each carrier has its own API, its own label format and its own idea of an address. We hide all of that behind one screen, so that a shop selling candles from a spare room can print a label in ten seconds and get on with the day.

We're a team of sixteen, with six engineers. Everyone works across the product, from the carrier integrations in Rails to the React screens shop owners use, and everyone talks to customers.

## What you'll do

- Build and maintain carrier integrations in Rails, and the tests that tell us when a carrier changed something.
- Build the screens shop owners use to buy labels, track parcels and handle returns, in React.
- Decide with the team what to fix when a carrier changes something overnight, and fix it.
- Improve how we validate and correct addresses, which saves our customers more money than anything else we do.
- Take your turn on support, one day a fortnight.

## About you

- Experience with Rails or a similar framework, and with React.
- You stay calm reading someone else's API documentation, however it's written.
- You like small, careful changes, and you ship often.
- You care about the person on the other end, who is usually in a hurry.

## Nice to have

- Experience with e-commerce platforms such as Shopify or WooCommerce.
- You've worked in logistics, or sold things online yourself.

## How we work

Remote in the UK or Ireland, with a stand-up three times a week and the rest in writing. Once a month we spend a day at the warehouse we use to test labels, printing and posting parcels to ourselves.

## What we offer

- 27 days off, plus public holidays.
- A share option scheme, explained properly, with the numbers.
- A pension with a 5% employer contribution.
- A home-office budget of £1,000.

## How we hire

1. A call with one of our founders.
2. A pairing session on a real (small) carrier integration.
3. A conversation with two engineers and someone from support.
