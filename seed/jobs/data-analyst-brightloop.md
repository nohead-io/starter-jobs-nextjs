We have three years of data on how teachers move through our courses: what they watch, what they skip, which assignments they hand in and when they give up. We use about a tenth of it. Most of our decisions about courses are still made on instinct, and some of our instincts are wrong.

Brightloop has around sixty thousand teachers learning with us, mostly in the United States, Canada and Brazil. You'll join a data team of two, our data engineer and our head of product, and work most closely with the course team who write and improve the lessons.

## What you'll do

- Build the dashboards the course team uses every week, and retire the ones nobody opens.
- Answer questions like "do shorter videos help?" properly, with experiments when we can run them.
- Find where teachers abandon a course, and work with the course team on why.
- Keep our data models tidy and documented with our data engineer.
- Share one finding a month with the whole company, in writing.

## About you

- Strong SQL; dbt and a BI tool of your choice.
- Enough statistics to run an A/B test and know when not to trust one.
- You can explain a result to people who don't like charts.
- You ask what decision a question is for before you answer it.

## Nice to have

- Python for the analyses SQL can't do.
- You've taught, or worked in education.

## How we work

Remote in the Americas, with core hours from 11:00 to 15:00 Eastern time. We write things down and keep meetings short. The whole company meets once a year, usually somewhere with a good school to visit.

## What we offer

- Health insurance, or a stipend where that makes more sense.
- 25 days off, plus the holidays where you live.
- A home-office budget of $1,500 to start, and $500 a year after.
- Every Brightloop course for free, which you'll have opinions about.

## How we hire

1. A call with our head of product.
2. A short analysis of a real (anonymised) dataset, talked through together. We pay for your time.
3. A conversation with the course team.
