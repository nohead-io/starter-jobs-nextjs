Until now the founders have decided what we build. It worked while we were small: we answered support ourselves and knew every customer's name. Parcelwise now ships for four thousand shops, and we'd like someone who decides what comes next better than we do.

You'll be our first product lead, reporting to our CEO and working with six engineers, a designer and our support team. The roadmap is yours, and so is the job of saying why.

## What you'll do

- Own the roadmap, and the reasons behind it, written down where everyone can read them.
- Talk to shops every week, and turn what you hear into decisions.
- Decide which carriers we add next, and which features we stop building.
- Work out our pricing with the founders, now that our customers range from one parcel a week to a thousand a day.
- Hire a product manager in your first year, and set up how product works here.

## About you

- Several years leading product at a software company that sells to small businesses.
- You've said no to a big customer for a good reason, and can explain why.
- You use data where it helps and talk to people where it doesn't.
- You write clearly, and you change your mind when you should.

## Nice to have

- Experience in logistics, e-commerce or payments.
- You've led product through a change in pricing.

## How we work

Remote in the UK or Ireland. We plan in six-week cycles, write a short document for every bigger decision, and meet in person for two days at the start of each quarter.

## What we offer

- Share options, at a level that reflects joining as our first product lead.
- 27 days off, plus public holidays.
- A pension with a 5% employer contribution.
- A monthly day at the warehouse we use to test labels.

## How we hire

1. A call with our CEO.
2. A conversation with the team about a product decision you've made, and what you'd do differently.
3. A paid half-day: interview three of our customers with us, and tell us what you heard.
4. References, and an offer.
