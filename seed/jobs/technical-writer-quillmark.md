We sell a writing tool, so our own writing gets read closely. Customers notice a clumsy sentence in our help centre, and developers notice an API example that doesn't run. We're looking for someone to own the help centre and the guides for our API, three days a week.

Today our engineers and our support team write the documentation between other work, and it shows. You'll be our first writer, working with support, the two engineers who build our API, and our designers when a feature is still being shaped.

## What you'll do

- Write and maintain the help centre, in Quillmark itself.
- Write the API guides with the engineers who build the API, and make sure every example runs.
- Read every support conversation that ends with "the docs didn't say", and fix the docs.
- Review the words in the product with our designers: buttons, errors, empty states.
- Set up a simple style guide, and help the rest of us follow it.

## About you

- You've written documentation people actually used, and can show us some.
- You can read code well enough to check an example works.
- You write short, plain sentences, and you cut more than you add.
- You're happy to own something on your own, three days a week.

## Nice to have

- Experience documenting an API for developers.
- You've used Quillmark, or a tool like it, for long documents.

## How we work

Three days a week, remote in Europe. You choose your days, as long as we agree on them. We work mostly in writing, with one planning call a week, and meet as a company three times a year.

## What we offer

- A salary for three days a week. The range above is for those three days.
- Pro-rata 28 days off, plus public holidays where you live.
- €1,000 a year for learning.
- A four-day week in August, which for you is two days.

## How we hire

1. A call with our head of support.
2. An edit of a real help article, and a talk about the changes you made.
3. A conversation with an engineer and a designer.
