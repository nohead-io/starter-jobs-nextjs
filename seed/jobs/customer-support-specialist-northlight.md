Most people who write to us are in the middle of a working day, with a patient waiting. A receptionist can't find tomorrow's bookings; a therapist's video call won't connect. They need a clear answer quickly, and they need it to be right.

Northlight is used by about three thousand small clinics in nine countries, from physiotherapists to speech therapists. Our support team of six answers every question within two hours during the working day, and we'd like to keep it that way as we grow in Germany and Austria.

## What you'll do

- Answer customers by email and chat, in English and German.
- Help new clinics move their bookings over from paper or another system, on a call when it's easier.
- Find out what's really wrong when a calendar misbehaves, and reproduce it for an engineer.
- Keep our help centre in German accurate, and write new articles when the same question comes in twice.
- Tell the product team what keeps coming up, with examples and numbers.

## About you

- Fluent English and German, written and spoken.
- Patient, precise and calm when someone isn't.
- You're curious about how software works, and don't stop at the first answer.
- Experience in customer support, ideally for software used at work.

## Nice to have

- You've worked in a clinic, or know how one runs.
- Another European language.

## How we work

Remote in Europe, working hours 8:00 to 16:30 CET, with one late shift a week until 18:00 on a rota. We work in pairs for your first month, and every new person in the company spends a week in support, so you'll never be alone with a hard problem.

## What we offer

- 28 days off, plus public holidays where you live.
- A laptop, a good headset and a second screen.
- €1,000 a year for learning, and German or English lessons if you want them.
- Two meetups a year, in Copenhagen or Berlin.

## How we hire

1. A short call with our support lead.
2. A written exercise: answers to three real (anonymised) questions, in both languages.
3. A conversation with two people from the support team.
