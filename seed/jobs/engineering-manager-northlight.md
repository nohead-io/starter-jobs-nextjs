Northlight's scheduling team builds the part of the product clinics use all day: the calendar, bookings, reminders and the video calls. Six engineers, one designer and a product manager. Their current manager is moving to lead our new platform team, and we're looking for the person who takes over.

The next year is a big one. We're moving three thousand clinics from our old calendar to a new one, one country at a time, while they keep booking patients every few minutes. It has to feel like nothing happened.

## What you'll do

- Lead and grow the team: one-to-ones, feedback, hiring and helping people do their best work.
- Keep delivery steady while we move clinics onto the new calendar, and decide with the team when a country is ready.
- Work with security and compliance: we handle health data in nine countries, each with its own rules.
- Hire two more engineers in your first year.
- Look after on call and incidents for the team, and make both calmer.

## About you

- You've managed a team of engineers before, and still enjoy reading code.
- You've shipped software that people depend on during their working day.
- You give clear feedback, kindly, and you ask for it too.
- You can explain a technical trade-off to someone who runs a clinic.

## Nice to have

- Experience with health data, or another regulated field.
- Danish, though our working language is English.

## How we work

Hybrid: two days a week in our Copenhagen office by the lakes, Tuesdays and Thursdays, and the rest from home. The team works core hours from 10:00 to 15:00, and nobody is expected to answer messages in the evening.

## What we offer

- A pension of 10% on top of the salary.
- Six weeks off, plus a day off on your birthday.
- Lunch in the office, and a bike if you want one.
- Help with moving to Denmark, from the paperwork to finding a flat.

## How we hire

1. A call with our VP of engineering.
2. A conversation about how you've led a team through something hard.
3. Meeting the team: an hour with three engineers and the designer.
4. A final talk with our CEO, and an offer within a week.
