Fernhill keeps the books for eleven thousand freelancers. Every invoice, expense and bank transaction ends up in one double-entry ledger, and the ledger has to be right to the cent, every time. Our customers file their taxes from it, and some of them have ten years of history in it.

You'll join a backend team of four, working in Ruby on Postgres. The ledger service is the heart of the product, and we're looking for someone to own it: to know why it works the way it does, and to decide how it changes.

## What you'll do

- Own the ledger service and the bank feeds that write to it.
- Make month-end reports fast for customers with ten years of history.
- Design how the ledger handles what's coming: VAT in two more countries, and multiple currencies.
- Review code with the four other backend engineers, and be on call one week in five.
- Help the support team when a customer's numbers don't add up, and find out why.

## About you

- You've run a Ruby or Python service in production for several years.
- You know Postgres well enough to read a query plan without a search engine.
- You'd rather write a migration twice than lose a cent once.
- You write tests that explain what the code is for.

## Nice to have

- Experience with accounting, banking or payments systems.
- You've worked with open banking APIs and their surprises.

## How we work

Remote anywhere in Europe within two hours of Dublin time. We work mostly in writing, with one planning call a week. Changes to the ledger get a short design document and two reviewers, and we've never regretted the time.

## What we offer

- 30 days off, plus public holidays where you live.
- A yearly team week in the west of Ireland.
- €1,500 a year for learning.
- An accountant (ours) for your own taxes.

## How we hire

1. A call with our CTO.
2. A pairing session on a part of the ledger, with one of the backend engineers.
3. A design conversation: how you'd add a second currency to a ledger like ours.
