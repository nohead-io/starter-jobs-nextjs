Our courses are written by teachers, for teachers. That's why they work in a classroom, but teachers aren't course designers, and a lesson that works in a room doesn't always work in a twelve-minute video. You'll work with them to turn their experience into lessons that work online.

Brightloop has around sixty thousand teachers learning with us, and our certificates are recognised by schools in four countries. This contract covers four new courses for next autumn: two on classroom management, one on teaching reading, and one on using assessment well.

## What you'll do

- Design four courses with their teacher-authors, from outline to assignments.
- Shape each lesson: what's a video, what's reading, what's practice, and what gets assessed.
- Write the assignment briefs and the rubrics our reviewers mark against.
- Test lessons with a small group of teachers before they go live, and change what doesn't work.
- Improve our course template with what you learn, for the authors who come after.

## About you

- Experience in instructional or learning design, ideally for adults.
- You can work with experts who know more about the subject than you, and help them say it simply.
- Organised enough to run four courses at once, with four different authors.
- Teaching experience helps a lot.

## Nice to have

- Experience with assessment and certification.
- Spanish or Portuguese.

## How we work

Remote anywhere, with two calls a week with the course team and the rest in writing. Authors are in North and South America, so some calls will be late in Europe or early in Asia.

## What we offer

- A six-month contract, extendable if we all want to.
- A salary at the yearly rate above, paid monthly.
- Every Brightloop course for free.

## How we hire

1. A call with our head of courses.
2. A walk-through of a course or lesson you've designed.
3. A conversation with one of our teacher-authors.
