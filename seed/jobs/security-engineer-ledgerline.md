We hold the most sensitive data a company has about its people: salaries, bank details, tax numbers and, in some countries, health and family information too. Forty thousand employees at fourteen hundred companies are paid through Ledgerline every month. Keeping their data safe is the job.

We're a team of thirty-five, and twelve of us are engineers. Until now, security has been everyone's part-time job, led by our CTO. You'll be the first person whose whole job it is, with the backing to change how we work.

## What you'll do

- Review designs and code with the engineering teams, early enough that your advice is cheap to follow.
- Run our security programme: the yearly audit, penetration tests, and the findings after, through to the fix.
- Improve how we handle secrets, access and logging, starting with who can read production data and why.
- Own our response when something goes wrong, from the first alert to the letter customers read.
- Answer the security questionnaires larger customers send, and turn the repeated questions into public documentation.
- Teach: a short session for new engineers, and the occasional write-up when something interesting turns up.

## About you

- Several years in application or cloud security at a software company.
- You can read our code (TypeScript and Go) and find what's wrong with it.
- You explain risks in plain words, and pick fixes people will actually use.
- You know when "good enough for now" is the right answer, and you write down what's left.

## Nice to have

- Experience with SOC 2 or ISO 27001, from the inside.
- Some knowledge of payroll, banking or other regulated data.

## How we work

Everyone works remotely, across seven countries in Europe, with most of the day overlapping. Decisions are written down: a short document, comments, then a call only if it's needed. We meet in person twice a year, for a week each time.

## What we offer

- A salary from our published bands, the same for everyone at the same level.
- 30 days off, plus public holidays where you live.
- €1,500 a year for learning, and the certifications you want.
- The laptop of your choice, and a budget for your home office.
- Payroll that's never late. We check.

## How we hire

1. A 30-minute call with our CTO, about you and the role.
2. A review of a small pull request we've prepared, talked through together. No take-home work.
3. A conversation with two engineers and a product manager.
4. References, and an offer within a week of the last step.
