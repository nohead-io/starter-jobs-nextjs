Growers use Loam outdoors, often far from a signal, sometimes with wet hands or gloves on. They record what they planted, where, and what they harvested, and at the end of the year that becomes the paperwork their organic certification asks for. The app has to work offline, all day, and get every record home when it can.

We're a team of nine, and three of us build the app. You'd be the second mobile engineer, working with our other mobile engineer and a backend engineer who looks after sync on the server.

## What you'll do

- Build our React Native app for iOS and Android.
- Own offline sync, conflicts included: two people harvesting the same bed on two phones.
- Make the app work with big buttons, bright sunlight and one free hand.
- Keep it fast on the cheap Android phones many growers carry.
- Visit a farm once a season to see the app used for real.

## About you

- Experience shipping a mobile app to both stores.
- You've thought hard about offline data, or want to.
- You test on real devices, not only the simulator.
- You enjoy talking to the people who use what you build.

## Nice to have

- Experience with SQLite or another local database on mobile.
- You grow vegetables, or have worked on a farm.

## How we work

Remote in Europe, with most of the team in Bristol and a few days in the office together each quarter. We plan around the growing season: big changes ship in winter, and from April we keep things steady.

## What we offer

- 30 days off, plus public holidays where you live.
- A test phone of each kind our customers use.
- £1,000 a year for learning.
- Wellies provided.

## How we hire

1. A call with our CTO.
2. A pairing session on a part of the app, with our mobile engineer.
3. A conversation with the team, in Bristol if you can make it, and on a call if not.
